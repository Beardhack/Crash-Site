# 02 - Cryo Annex / Remaining Sleepers

## Prompt

Create a 16:9 pixel-art point-and-click adventure background showing a **Cryo Annex with remaining sleepers**, viewed through a fixed wall-mounted CCTV camera high in one corner. The room is narrower and more claustrophobic than the main cryo chamber. Nine cryosleep pods line the walls in two staggered rows, all closed and faintly glowing blue-white through frosted glass. The sleepers are obscured by condensation, never clearly visible. A central power bus runs along the floor with thick cables feeding the pods. A small annex sleep-monitor terminal glows on the right wall with abstract green/amber status blocks, not readable text. Near the lower-left wall is a clipped emergency manual crank, clearly visible as a small industrial tool. An empty auxiliary power cradle sits near the pod bus, ready for a power cell.

The mood is ethical pressure, not horror: the pods look fragile, dependent, and human. Use cold blue cryo light, weak red emergency spill, frost buildup, dust on sealed glass, old labels, taped maintenance notes that are not readable. Pixel-art CCTV style with scanlines, slight barrel distortion, dirty lens specks, low-light noise. No surrounding game UI, no overlay, no timestamp, no captions, no visible player character.

## Important Visual Anchors

- Rows of viable sleeper pods.
- Wall sleep-monitor terminal.
- Manual crank clipped to wall.
- Empty auxiliary power cradle near floor cables.
- Clear exit back toward the main cryo chamber.

## Negative Prompt

No alien bodies, no creature silhouettes, no gore, no readable lore text, no UI frame, no first-person hands, no clean luxury sci-fi, no bright heroic lighting.
