# 04 - Broken Research Lab

## Prompt

Create a 16:9 pixel-art point-and-click adventure background of a **Broken Research Lab** inside a damaged off-world terraforming research station, viewed through a fixed ceiling-corner CCTV camera. The room contains overturned lab benches, cracked sample containers, ordinary rock and engineered soil trays, dust-coated instruments, broken glass, and a damaged observation window looking into a cleaner sealed room beyond. A wall terminal on the left still flickers with static and abstract budget/status blocks, not readable text. A ceramic fuse array is visibly wedged under a collapsed equipment rack near the floor, catching a tiny amber glint. Red dust has entered through cracks, coating everything. Exposed cables hang from the ceiling. The lab should communicate failed mission design and corporate/scientific pressure, not alien mystery.

Style: old-school pixel-art adventure background, modern polish, CCTV/security-camera feed. Use low-resolution pixel clusters, CRT scanlines, dirty lens, emergency red lighting mixed with cold teal monitor glow, slight barrel distortion, deep shadows. No game UI, no inventory, no cursor, no readable overlay, no timestamp, no captions, no visible player character.

## Important Visual Anchors

- Flickering lab terminal.
- Collapsed rack with visible fuse array.
- Cracked observation window.
- Broken sample trays with normal soil/rock, not alien artifacts.
- Clear exit to decontamination hall and implied clean room door.

## Negative Prompt

No alien specimens, no monster eggs, no glowing magic rocks, no weapons display, no readable text, no UI frame, no clean pristine lab, no photoreal render.
