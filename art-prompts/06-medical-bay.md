# 06 - Medical Bay

## Prompt

Create a 16:9 pixel-art point-and-click adventure background showing a damaged off-world station **Medical Bay**, viewed through a fixed ceiling-corner CCTV camera. The room is small, practical, and too quiet: a treatment chair sits near the center under a dead surgical lamp, its restraint straps cut and hanging loose. Sterile wrappers and paper labels drift across the floor whenever damaged vents cough. A wall medical kiosk glows with abstract green/white triage blocks, not readable text, still politely asking for patients who are gone. An empty medication fridge stands open on the left, cold fog gone, shelves bare except for curled apology labels and broken plastic bins. Cabinets are dented, drawers pulled open, and a rolling tray has tipped sterile tools onto the scratched floor.

The scene should feel like a care system that kept trying after care was no longer possible, mournful but not gory. Use grounded NASA/industrial medical design: hard plastic panels, rubber seals, washable wall surfaces, practical storage, clipped tubing, old warning stripes, dust in seams, weak teal medical light mixed with red emergency spill. Pixel-art CCTV style with subtle scanlines, dirty lens specks, slight wide-angle distortion, low-resolution pixel clusters, desaturated shadows. No surrounding game UI, no inventory, no cursor, no timestamp, no captions, no readable overlay, no visible player character.

## Important Visual Anchors

- Treatment chair with cut restraint straps.
- Glowing medical kiosk as the obvious terminal.
- Open empty medical fridge.
- Sterile wrappers and apology labels scattered on the floor.
- Clear exits toward decontamination hall and mess/rec room.

## Negative Prompt

No gore, no bodies, no monsters, no zombie hospital imagery, no fantasy healing pods, no glossy cyberpunk clinic, no readable medical text, no UI frame, no human character, no photorealistic rendering.
