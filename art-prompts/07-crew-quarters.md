# 07 - Crew Quarters

## Prompt

Create a 16:9 pixel-art point-and-click adventure background showing abandoned **Crew Quarters** inside a damaged off-world research station, viewed through a fixed high corner CCTV camera. Personal bunks line the walls in a compact shared room. Emergency red light makes ordinary family photos, paper keepsakes, and taped snapshots look fragile and strange. One locker near the left side is already open: inside, a cracked command access card hangs from a photo strip, clearly visible as a small pickup object. A folded apology note rests on a bunk or small desk in the lower-right area, lit by a thin beam from a failing task lamp. Bedding is rumpled, storage bins are half pulled out, socks and soft personal items are mixed with station-issued survival gear.

The mood is intimate human absence, not horror: a normal living space interrupted by corporate disaster. Use practical station materials, dull metal bunks, Velcro straps, scratched lockers, foam padding, dust, old coffee packets, taped photos, and emergency lighting. Pixel-art CCTV treatment with mild barrel distortion, scanlines, dirty lens, cold teal shadows, dim red alarm reflections, and low-resolution pixel detail. No game UI, no inventory bar, no cursor, no timestamp, no captions, no readable overlay, no visible player character.

## Important Visual Anchors

- Open Mira locker with access card and photo strip.
- Folded apology note as a secondary inspectable object.
- Wall of crew photos and personal keepsakes.
- Multiple compact bunks and lockers.
- Clear exits toward decontamination hall, mess/rec room, and power junction.

## Negative Prompt

No visible people, no corpses, no monsters, no luxury apartment styling, no bright cozy sitcom lighting, no readable diary text, no UI elements, no external game frame, no photoreal render.
