# 01 - Cryo Chamber

## Prompt

Create a 16:9 pixel-art point-and-click adventure background showing a damaged off-world research station **Cryo Chamber**, seen through a fixed ceiling-corner CCTV camera. The camera is mounted high near the room entrance, looking down at a row of cryosleep pods under dim red emergency lights. One pod is open in the foreground with frost spilling onto the floor, straps loose, vapor hanging in the air. Two or three nearby pods are dark and iced over. A small pod monitor glows green beside the open pod, with abstract unreadable vitals and flickering light. The room is industrial NASA-style: ribbed wall panels, exposed cables, warning stripes, scratched metal floor plates, condensation, cracked glass, hydraulic arms, emergency siren light reflecting on frost. The ceiling has damage and dust leaking from a seam. Mood: cold, quiet, newly awakened panic, human disaster.

Use retro CCTV/pixel-art treatment: subtle scanlines, dirty lens, slight wide-angle distortion, low-resolution pixel clusters, desaturated teal shadows and emergency red highlights. No game UI, no inventory bar, no cursor, no readable overlay, no timestamp, no captions, no visible player character.

## Important Visual Anchors

- Open cryo pod as the main focal point.
- Glowing pod monitor as an obvious interactable object.
- Frozen inactive pods as environmental storytelling.
- Exit doorway or decontamination door visible in the background.
- Floor warning markings and cables to support point-and-click readability.

## Negative Prompt

No aliens, no monsters, no fantasy tech, no glossy spaceship bridge, no clean futuristic showroom, no text overlay, no UI frame, no readable dialogue, no human character standing in frame, no marketing poster composition.
