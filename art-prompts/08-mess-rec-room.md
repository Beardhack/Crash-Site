# 08 - Mess / Rec Room

## Prompt

Create a 16:9 pixel-art point-and-click adventure background showing a damaged station **Mess / Rec Room**, viewed through a fixed wall-mounted CCTV camera high near one corner. The space is half cafeteria and half tiny theater: bolted tables, overturned cups, meal trays, ration packets, a scuffed floor, and a wide rec screen paused on an Earth ocean image that throws soft blue light across the room. The screen image should feel recognizable as ocean and horizon but contain no readable captions or UI. Along one wall is an empty ration dispenser or ration storage rack, stripped almost clean except for one small magnet or note shape that is not readable. A few simple recreation objects remain: a strapped-down board game, loose cards, headphones, and a cracked mug floating slightly off its Velcro pad.

The room should tell the story of people trying to build a home inside a machine. Keep it grounded and worn: industrial cafeteria fixtures, dull plastic seating, metal brackets, scratched wall panels, condensation, red dust creeping through floor seams, cold blue screen glow mixed with weak amber emergency lights. Pixel-art CCTV style with scanlines, dirty lens specks, slight wide-angle distortion, low-light pixel noise. No surrounding game UI, no inventory, no cursor, no timestamp, no readable overlay, no captions, no visible player character.

## Important Visual Anchors

- Paused rec screen showing Earth ocean in blue light.
- Empty ration wall or dispenser.
- Overturned cups, meal trays, and bolted tables.
- Small human recreation objects.
- Clear exits toward crew quarters, medical bay, and comms room.

## Negative Prompt

No readable movie subtitles, no lively people, no monsters, no alien cafeteria, no neon cyberpunk bar, no bright restaurant design, no UI frame, no first-person hands, no photorealistic rendering.
