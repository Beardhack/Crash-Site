# 09 - Comms Room

## Prompt

Create a 16:9 pixel-art point-and-click adventure background showing a damaged off-world station **Comms Room**, viewed through a fixed ceiling-corner CCTV camera. The room is packed with practical relay racks, cable bundles, wall-mounted signal hardware, and a main comms console that still glows through static. The console sits near the center-left with abstract waveform blocks, amber error bars, and green signal traces, but no readable text. A cracked reinforced glass wall or viewport on the right looks out onto a dead antenna mast bent toward the red dust flats outside. The glass is scratched and spiderwebbed, with red dust collected in the lower seal. Loose headset pieces, a coil of cable, and a fallen maintenance panel sit on the floor.

The scene should feel like a room that can still listen but barely speak: static, beacon math, and one weak signal beyond the station. Use grounded industrial sci-fi design: heavy equipment cabinets, analog fallback knobs, practical antenna routing, taped cable labels that are too degraded to read, dust, weak teal console glow, red emergency spill, and dim exterior storm haze through the window. Pixel-art CCTV style with subtle scanlines, dirty lens, mild barrel distortion, desaturated shadows, low-resolution pixel clusters. No game UI, no inventory, no cursor, no timestamp, no captions, no readable overlay, no visible player character.

## Important Visual Anchors

- Main comms console with abstract static and signal graphics.
- Cracked antenna window looking toward the bent mast.
- Relay racks and cable bundles.
- Fallen headset or maintenance gear.
- Clear exits toward research lab, mess/rec room, and rover bay.

## Negative Prompt

No holographic command bridge, no glossy spaceship cockpit, no readable radio text, no monsters, no aliens, no military weapons display, no UI frame, no character in frame, no photoreal rendering.
