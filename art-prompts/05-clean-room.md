# 05 - Clean Room

## Prompt

Create a 16:9 pixel-art point-and-click adventure background showing a damaged but still partially sterile **Clean Room** connected to a research lab, viewed through a fixed CCTV camera high in the corner behind protective glass. The room is brighter and colder than the broken lab: white-gray wall panels, sealed cabinets, a central clean table, sample trays, dust streaks where pressure seals failed, and small air jets still puffing from wall vents. One emergency wall panel is jammed open just slightly or visibly crank-operated, with a square manual socket. Inside or near that panel is pressure sealant foam as a clear pickup object. The room should look like a sterile process trying to continue despite structural failure. Red dust has invaded in thin lines across the floor and cabinet seams.

Use grounded industrial sci-fi design, not sleek space opera: practical cabinets, rubber seals, warning stripes, scratched acrylic, old maintenance hardware. Pixel-art CCTV style with slight wide-angle distortion, scanlines, dirty lens, teal-white fluorescent light, weak red emergency reflections, low-resolution pixel texture. No surrounding game UI, no inventory, no cursor, no timestamp, no readable overlay, no captions, no visible player character.

## Important Visual Anchors

- Jammed emergency wall panel with manual crank socket.
- Sealant foam canister visible as pickup item.
- Sterile central table and sample cabinets.
- Air jets/mist as subtle machine-life cue.
- Door/window connection back to broken research lab.

## Negative Prompt

No aliens, no biological horror, no futuristic hologram UI, no readable text, no external game interface, no human character, no clean showroom lighting, no photorealistic rendering.
