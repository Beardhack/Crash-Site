# 03 - Decontamination Hall

## Prompt

Create a 16:9 pixel-art point-and-click adventure background showing a damaged **Decontamination Hall** in an off-world research station, seen through an old fixed CCTV camera mounted high at the end of the corridor. The hall has two heavy industrial doors, one leading back toward cryo and one deeper into the station. Automated air jets along the walls misfire into cracks in the ceiling, creating short bursts of mist. The floor is wet in places, with hazard stripes, grated drains, scattered sterile wrappers, and red dust blown in from damaged vents. Wall panels are scratched and dented. Some overhead lights are dead; others flicker amber. A mission schedule board or wall panel is visible but too degraded to read.

The scene should feel transitional and functional: a place built for procedure that has kept running after the procedure stopped making sense. Use grounded NASA/industrial materials, old rubber seals, metal bulkheads, plastic curtain remnants, pressure doors, and emergency red/amber light. Pixel-art CCTV view, slight wide-angle corridor perspective, scanlines, dirty lens, muted teal shadows, red dust haze. No surrounding game UI, no cursor, no inventory, no timestamp, no captions, no readable text overlay, no visible player character.

## Important Visual Anchors

- Strong corridor perspective.
- Air jets/mist as subtle animated-candidate machine detail.
- Two or more clear exits/doors.
- Hazard-striped floor and drains.
- Wall schedule/panel as an inspectable visual object.

## Negative Prompt

No alien slime, no monsters, no battle damage from weapons, no glossy cyberpunk neon, no UI elements, no readable labels, no character posing in frame.
