# 10 - Power Junction

## Prompt

Create a 16:9 pixel-art point-and-click adventure background showing a damaged station **Power Junction**, viewed through a fixed high wall-mounted CCTV camera. The room is narrow and vertical, with breaker nests climbing the walls in dark industrial columns. Thick conduits run under grated floor panels and up into ceiling trays. The central breaker nest has a clearly visible empty ceramic fuse slot, scorched around the edges, waiting for a missing fuse array. A life-support panel on the left glows weakly with abstract wattage blocks and warning color bands, not readable text. Floor-level alarm lights pulse under dust. One corridor door leads back toward cryo, another toward crew quarters, one heavy service hatch leads to the reactor/fuel collector, and a blocked command corridor door sits under fallen ceiling debris.

The mood is infrastructure under pressure: patient alarms, bad math, and a building trying to keep breathing. Use grounded NASA/industrial station materials: breaker cabinets, removable metal panels, caution stripes, cable trays, heat discoloration, bolted junction boxes, old labels that cannot be read, red dust, weak teal electrical light, amber warning glows, and emergency red reflections. Pixel-art CCTV treatment with scanlines, dirty lens specks, slight wide-angle distortion, low-resolution pixel detail, and desaturated shadows. No surrounding game UI, no inventory bar, no cursor, no timestamp, no captions, no readable overlay, no visible player character.

## Important Visual Anchors

- Main breaker nest with empty scorched fuse-array slot.
- Weak life-support panel on the left wall.
- Thick floor and ceiling conduits.
- Reactor/fuel collector service hatch.
- Blocked command corridor door under debris.

## Negative Prompt

No magical electricity, no giant glowing reactor core, no action-movie explosions, no weapon damage, no readable schematics, no UI elements, no human character, no clean futuristic showroom, no photorealistic rendering.
