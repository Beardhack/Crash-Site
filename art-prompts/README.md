# Crash Site Art Prompts

These prompts are for generating **room background art only**. Do not include the browser game UI, inventory bar, buttons, text boxes, labels, cursor, hover highlights, or surrounding interface frame.

## Global Visual Direction

Use this shared direction for every scene:

- 16:9 static point-and-click adventure background.
- Old-school pixel-art adventure game, but polished and cinematic.
- Fixed CCTV/security camera viewpoint inside a damaged off-world research station.
- Slight wide-angle lens, high corner or wall-mounted perspective, mild barrel distortion.
- Grounded NASA/industrial sci-fi, not space opera.
- Human-centered disaster: emergency lighting, dust, frost, cracked glass, abandoned tools, corporate research infrastructure, evidence of rushed evacuation.
- Retro CRT/CCTV image character: low-resolution pixel detail, subtle scanline texture, slight chromatic fringing, dim exposure, dirty lens, desaturated shadows.
- No readable UI overlay, no timestamp, no HUD, no text captions. Environmental signage may exist but should not dominate or need to be readable.
- No aliens, monsters, weapons-forward composition, or fantasy elements.
- No visible player character.
- Leave the room readable for gameplay: clear silhouettes for important interactable objects, strong focal points, visible exits/doors, enough negative space for hotspots.

## Suggested Output

- Aspect ratio: 16:9.
- Preferred size: 1920x1080, 1536x864, or 1024x576.
- Style: pixel art / pixel-painted background, not vector art, not 3D render, not photoreal.
- Composition: room background only, edge-to-edge image.
